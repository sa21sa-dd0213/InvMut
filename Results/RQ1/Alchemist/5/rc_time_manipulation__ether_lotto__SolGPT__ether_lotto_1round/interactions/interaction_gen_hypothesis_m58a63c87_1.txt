**Original code:**  
The random number is generated using `block.timestamp` and `block.difficulty`.  
`block.difficulty` was a value that changed with mining difficulty — unpredictable to a degree.

**Mutant code:**  
Replaces `block.difficulty` with `block.prevrandao`.  
`block.prevrandao` was introduced after the Ethereum Merge (PoS transition). On a PoS chain, `block.prevrandao` is a pseudo-random value provided by the beacon chain, which is **deterministic for the same block** and known to validators/block proposers.

In the original, on a PoW chain, `block.difficulty` varies and is not easily manipulated.  
In the mutant, `block.prevrandao` is **reproducible and known before the transaction is included**, meaning an attacker could predict the outcome of the random draw and choose to play only when they will win.
