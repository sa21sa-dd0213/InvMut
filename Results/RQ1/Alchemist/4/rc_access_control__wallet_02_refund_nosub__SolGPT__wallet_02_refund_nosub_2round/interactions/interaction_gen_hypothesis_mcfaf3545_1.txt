The original `withdraw` function has:

```solidity
require(amount <= balances[msg.sender]);
```

This means the user can only withdraw an amount up to (and including) their entire balance. That's the normal, safe behavior.

The mutant changes `<=` to `>=`:

```solidity

```

Now the user is **required** to withdraw at least their entire balance or more. If they try to withdraw less than their full balance, the transaction reverts.
