3. **Key difference**:  
   The original uses `block.timestamp` which is *not* a source of randomness—it’s controlled by the miner. The mutant uses `block.prevrandao`, which is a *better* but still manipulable pseudo-random value. However, for the purposes of mutation testing, the test must rely on the **observable output** being different between the two.
