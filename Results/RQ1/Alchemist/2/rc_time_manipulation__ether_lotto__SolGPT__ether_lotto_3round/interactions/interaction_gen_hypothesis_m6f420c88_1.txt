The original function `play()` works like this:
- Player sends 10 wei.
- 1 wei goes to the bank as a fee.
- The remaining pot (pot - 1) goes to the winner.
- Pot is then reset to 0.

The mutant changes `pot - FEE_AMOUNT` to `pot + FEE_AMOUNT`.  
This means the winner would receive more than the actual pot contains — specifically, they would get `pot + 1` instead of `pot - 1`.
