```solidity

```

This is a classic overflow guard: it ensures that adding `msg.value` to the existing balance doesn’t wrap around (overflow). It will always pass for any positive `msg.value` because `acc.balance + msg.value` is always ≥ `acc.balance` unless overflow occurs.
